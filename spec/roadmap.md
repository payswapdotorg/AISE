# AISE v2 Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Understand

BOQ Lens ingests BOQs and gives users source-traceable explanations, cost hierarchy, search, health checks and role-specific views.

## Phase 2 — Capture

Capability-aware guided capture produces measurable 2D/3D reality models from supported phones and sensors.

## Phase 3 — Connect

BOQ, drawings, specifications and observed reality become cross-linked through stable identifiers and evidence.

## Phase 4 — Diagnose

Structured Engineering Cases capture defects, evidence, measurements, uncertainty, hypotheses and review.

## Phase 5 — Simulate

Intervention Studio produces reproducible proposed states with synchronized 3D, 2D and BOQ layers.

## Phase 6 — Execute

Approved work is converted into inspectable work packages, quantities and evidence checkpoints.

## Phase 7 — Learn

Post-work captures and outcomes are linked to prior proposals and become benchmark/learning data.

## Phase 8 — Platform

AISE becomes the primary interface across incumbents, with enterprise connectors, APIs, permissions and developer tooling.
