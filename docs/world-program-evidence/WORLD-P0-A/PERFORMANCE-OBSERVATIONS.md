## 1. glTF parse throughput (REAL parser, generated GLB fixtures)

JSON-only GLB containers generated by the harness (no BIN chunk — the
measurement targets JSON-document traversal + container validation; buffer
arithmetic is validated against DECLARED sizes).

| fixture | JSON bytes | runs | median ms | MB/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m10 v100 i240 | 2,476 | 25 | 0.131 | 18.3 |
| m50 v400 i1200 | 11,648 | 25 | 0.207 | 53.9 |
| m200 v1000 i3000 | 46,804 | 25 | 0.450 | 99.1 |
| m800 v1000 i3000 | 188,404 | 25 | 1.829 | 98.2 |

Observation (measured, not projected): throughput rises with document size
and plateaus near ~99 MB/s for this validator's full structural pass —
consistent with per-object overhead dominating small documents. A real
production asset mixes far larger accessor counts per byte; the WORLD-P1
protocol (below) re-measures on the Khronos sample corpus.
